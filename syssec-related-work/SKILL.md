# syssec-related-work

Use `syssec-related-work` to write a useful related work section instead of a
citation dump.

## Inputs

- related-work map
- baseline matrix
- claim set

## Outputs

- related-work section plan
- grouped comparison narrative
- omission warnings

## Workflow

1. Group prior work by mechanism or study style.
2. Compare assumptions and goals against the current work.
3. Explain overlap, difference, and boundary honestly.
4. Surface near-neighbor papers that deserve explicit mention.
5. Route the section to writing.

## Boundaries

- Do not claim novelty by ignoring close work.
- Do not compare unrelated threat models as if they were peers.
