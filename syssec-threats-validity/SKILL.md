# syssec-threats-validity

Use `syssec-threats-validity` to state what the study does not prove.

## Inputs

- evaluation outputs
- compatibility and performance notes
- study scope

## Outputs

- validity notes
- limitation list
- overclaim warning list

## Workflow

1. Review assumptions against what was actually tested.
2. Identify internal validity risks.
3. Identify external generalization risks.
4. Identify construct and measurement risks.
5. Route the cleaned claim boundary to synthesis and writing.

## Boundaries

- Do not treat this as optional polish.
- If the threat list collapses a core claim, route back upstream.
