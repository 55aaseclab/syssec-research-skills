# syssec-write

Use `syssec-write` to turn the claim map into a coherent paper draft.

## Inputs

- claim-evidence map
- related-work map
- results and figure plan
- target venue constraints

## Outputs

- `paper-outline.md`
- section drafts
- missing-evidence warnings

## Workflow

1. Define the paper spine around the claim set.
2. Draft the contribution, method, and evaluation flow.
3. Keep assumptions and caveats visible in the right sections.
4. Mark any paragraph that outruns the evidence.
5. Route to related-work, figures, or submission.

## Boundaries

- Writing must not invent validation.
- If a section needs missing evidence, route back instead of bluffing.
